SensorFit: Hands-Free Rep Counter

Your phone counts the reps.

SensorFit is a hands-free rep counter that turns your Android phone into a push-up counter, squat counter, and sit-up counter. Place your phone, start a set, and SensorFit uses the proximity sensor and accelerometer to count automatically — no tapping mid-set, no smartwatch required. Voice counts reps. Works offline.

What it tracks

  • Push-ups — phone on the floor (proximity sensor)
  • Squats — phone in your pocket (accelerometer)
  • Sit-ups — phone on your chest (accelerometer)
  • Bench dips — phone on the bench (proximity)
  • Manual logging for any other exercise

Built for real training

  • Live rep counter with haptic feedback and optional voice count
  • Structured sets with countdown and rest
  • Workout log with share and edit
  • Stats plus daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly goals
  • Streak tracking
  • Works offline for counting
